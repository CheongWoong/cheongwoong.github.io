I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Graduate School of AI, KAIST, affiliated with the SAIL (advisor: Jaesik Choi). My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and explainable AI (XAI), with two main directions: (1) understanding how knowledge is encoded, processed, and utilized in language models, including how it is retrieved and extracted; and (2) investigating how that knowledge can be modified or augmented.
